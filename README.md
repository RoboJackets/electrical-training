# RoboJackets Electrical Training

**Please bookmark this page!**

**Also, be sure to visit the RoboJackets Firmware Training page as well:** https://github.com/RoboJackets/firmware-training

Welcome to the source repository for the 2025 RoboJackets Electrical training program!

## Training Schedule 2025

* Time: 6:30pm - 8:00pm on Mondays and Tuesdays
* Bring: You, Your computer, A separate soul to sacrifice

Here is a breakdown of what electrical training will be about week-to-week:
* Week 1: Introductions and electricity basics and Arduino
* Week 2: Electricity components and Kicad Parts and Libraries
* Week 3: Kicad Schematics
* Week 4: Fall Break (**NO** training this week)
* Week 5: Kicad Board Layout
* Ext. Week 5: Soldering Workshop @ The HIVE Makerspace<sup>*1,2*</sup>
* Week 6: Overflow training<sup>*3*</sup>

Note<sup>*1*</sup>: Ext. Week 5 is on a Friday, and y'all be able to get practice with both surface mount (SMD) and through-hole (THT) soldering.\
Note<sup>*2*</sup>: Check out the Hive's website to learn more about it: https://hive.ece.gatech.edu/about/ \
Note<sup>*3*</sup>: Week 6 <ins>most likely *won't*</ins> be taking place, but we will let y'all know ahead of time.
